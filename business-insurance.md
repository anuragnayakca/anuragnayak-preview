Insurance planning for business owners becomes clearer when the conversation begins with dependency rather than product type. Who does the business rely on? What obligation would arise if an owner died or became disabled? What debt or guarantee is connected to a particular person? What does a shareholder agreement require, and how would that requirement actually be funded?

Those questions can lead to different insurance considerations, but they can also reveal that a legal agreement, valuation, succession plan or emergency reserve needs attention first.

## Key-person considerations

A key person may be an owner, salesperson, technical specialist, executive or other individual whose absence would affect revenue, financing, operations or important relationships. Insurance may help provide liquidity after death or serious illness, depending on the policy and business need.

The business should not choose a coverage amount simply by applying a generic multiple. A more useful approach is to consider the financial effect of the loss, expected recovery period, replacement cost, debt obligations and the role the person plays.

## Buy-sell funding

A buy-sell or shareholder agreement may establish what happens to an owner's shares after a triggering event. Insurance can sometimes provide funding for death or other insured events, but the insurance must align with the legal agreement and valuation method.

The policy does not replace the agreement. The lawyer, accountant and insurance advisor each address different parts of the arrangement.

## Corporate-owned life insurance

Corporate ownership can be appropriate in some circumstances, including certain estate, succession or liquidity needs. It can also involve premium funding, policy values, adjusted cost basis, beneficiary arrangements and potential Capital Dividend Account considerations.

Those outcomes are not automatic. They depend on the policy structure, legislation and corporate circumstances, so the insurance discussion should be coordinated with the corporation's accountant and tax professionals.
