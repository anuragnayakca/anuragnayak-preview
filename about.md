Having spent more than two decades in corporate IT across technical and leadership roles, I understand how much attention a demanding career requires. There is always another project, greater responsibility, a new skill to develop or a career move to consider. Along the way, insurance, workplace benefits, savings and investment decisions can be made at different times and rarely reviewed together.

The challenge is not always a lack of income or savings. The more useful question is whether the different pieces still reflect your goals, responsibilities and the risks around your income.

Today, I help professionals, families and business owners step back, understand what they already have and make more deliberate decisions. My role is not to begin with a product. It is to identify what is already working, what may deserve attention and whether any change is appropriate.

## Particular familiarity with demanding professional careers

My background gives me particular familiarity with technology careers, workplace benefits, contract work, job changes and income-protection questions. At the same time, these issues are not limited to one profession. I work with professionals from different fields, along with families and business owners.

## How I approach a review

### Understand before recommending

We begin with your current arrangements, responsibilities and questions. If what you already have is appropriate, that is useful to know too.

### Explain without unnecessary jargon

The important features, conditions and trade-offs should be understandable before a decision is made.

### Consider the complete situation

Employment, family responsibilities, business ownership, debt, access to funds, risk tolerance and time horizon can all affect the discussion.

### Make decisions without pressure

A first conversation is intended to clarify whether there is something worth reviewing and whether I am the right person to help.
