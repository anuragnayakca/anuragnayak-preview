For many technology professionals, income depends primarily on the ability to keep working. That makes disability coverage worth understanding, whether the protection comes through an employer, an individual policy or a combination of both.

## Five details that matter

### Definition of disability
Policies can define disability in different ways. The wording determines the circumstances in which benefits may become payable.

### Waiting period
The elimination or waiting period is the time that generally must pass before eligible benefits begin. Emergency savings and employer sick leave can affect how this period fits into a plan.

### Benefit amount and maximums
Workplace plans may use a percentage of income but also apply monthly maximums. Higher-income professionals should check both numbers.

### Benefit period
Some benefits are payable for a limited period while others may continue longer, subject to continued eligibility and policy terms.

### What happens when employment changes
Employer coverage may change or end when you leave a job. Contractors and incorporated professionals may have a different benefits structure entirely.

The useful question is not simply "Do I have disability insurance?" It is "What would the coverage actually do in my situation?"
