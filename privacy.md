This privacy policy describes the website configuration and information-handling practices currently intended for this site. It should be updated whenever the services or data practices change.

## Information collected through the contact form

The general contact form is intended to collect only your name, email address, province, visitor type, topic of interest, a short message and consent to receive a response. A phone number is not required through the form.

Please do not submit medical records, Social Insurance Numbers, investment-account numbers, policy numbers, banking details, passwords, detailed financial balances or other sensitive information through the general website form.

## Why information is collected

Information submitted through the contact form is used to respond to your inquiry and, where appropriate, arrange a conversation. Inquiry-response consent is separate from any future marketing or newsletter consent.

## Scheduling provider

The website links to an external scheduling service at `booknow.anuragnayak.ca`. Information you provide there is processed under the scheduling provider's privacy terms and configuration.

## Hosting and form processing

The website is intended to be hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process technical request information needed to deliver and secure the site. Contact-form submissions are processed through Cloudflare Pages Functions and may be transmitted through a transactional email delivery provider so the inquiry can reach the business contact address.

## Analytics and cookies

No advertising pixels are installed by default. Privacy-conscious basic analytics may be enabled later. If non-essential tracking or marketing cookies are added, this policy and the site's consent controls must be updated before use.

## Retention and safeguards

Contact information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and any applicable legal, regulatory or recordkeeping obligations. Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure or loss.

## Access, correction and withdrawal of consent

To ask about personal information submitted through this website, request a correction or withdraw consent for future contact, email [{{ site.data.site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.site.email }}).

## Cross-border processing

Some service providers may process information outside your province or outside Canada. Where applicable, their privacy terms and safeguards govern that processing.
