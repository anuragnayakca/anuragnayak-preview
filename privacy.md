## Information you choose to provide
If you use the contact form, the website may collect your name, email address, optional phone number, province, inquiry topic and message. Please do not submit sensitive financial, medical or identification information through the public form.

## How information is used
Information submitted through the contact form is used to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about the matter you asked about.

## Website security and spam prevention
The site may use security services such as Cloudflare and Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent abuse and protect the website. These services may process technical information necessary to provide security functions.

## Analytics
If privacy-conscious analytics are enabled, they should be configured to measure website usage without sending sensitive financial information.

## Contact
Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to **contact@anuragnayak.ca**.
