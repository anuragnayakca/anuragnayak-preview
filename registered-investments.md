Registered accounts are often discussed as though one account is automatically the best place to save. In reality, the right structure depends on the purpose of the money, the time horizon, tax circumstances, access needs and available contribution room.

The account is only one decision. The investment held inside the account is another.

## RRSP

An RRSP is designed primarily for retirement savings and can provide a tax deduction for eligible contributions. Withdrawals are generally taxable, with specific programs and rules applying in certain circumstances. The value of an RRSP deduction can depend on current income, future income expectations and other tax considerations.

## TFSA

A TFSA allows eligible contributions without an upfront tax deduction, while qualifying withdrawals are generally not included in taxable income. Withdrawn amounts can generally create new contribution room in a later year, subject to current rules.

The flexibility can make a TFSA useful for different time horizons, but the investment selected inside the account should still reflect when the money may be needed.

## FHSA

The FHSA combines features intended to support eligible first-time home buyers. Contributions may be deductible and qualifying withdrawals may receive favourable tax treatment when conditions are met. Eligibility and current rules should always be confirmed through official sources.

## RESP and RDSP

RESPs are designed for education savings, while RDSPs are designed for eligible people with disabilities. Both can involve government incentives and detailed eligibility rules. Because those programs can change, official government information should be used for current contribution, grant and withdrawal rules.

## Official sources for current rules

For current eligibility, contribution and withdrawal rules, use Government of Canada sources rather than relying on static limits published on an advisor website:

- [Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP) — Canada Revenue Agency](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/registered-retirement-savings-plan-rrsp.html)
- [Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA) — Canada Revenue Agency](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/tax-free-savings-account.html)
- [First Home Savings Account (FHSA) — Canada Revenue Agency](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html)
- [Registered Education Savings Plan (RESP) — Canada Revenue Agency](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/registered-education-savings-plans-resps.html)
- [Registered Disability Savings Plan (RDSP) — Government of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/disability/savings.html)
