## Accessibility measures

The website includes semantic landmarks, a skip-to-content link, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus indicators, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, labelled form controls, descriptive links and contrast-aware colour choices.

Native HTML controls such as `details` and `summary` are used where practical for expandable content because they provide established keyboard and assistive-technology behaviour.

## Known limitations

External services such as the booking provider may have accessibility characteristics outside this website's direct control. If an external experience creates a barrier, please contact us and we will try to provide an alternative way to complete the task.
