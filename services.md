## Personal Insurance

Life, critical illness and disability insurance can be reviewed in the context of income, family responsibilities, debt, workplace coverage and the financial impact of an interruption to earning ability.

[Explore Personal Insurance →](/services/personal-insurance/)

## Business Insurance

Business insurance discussions may include owners, key people, buy-sell funding considerations, business obligations and continuity questions. Tax, legal and corporate-structure considerations should be coordinated with the appropriate professionals.

[Explore Business Insurance →](/services/business-insurance/)

## Employee Benefits

Group life, health, dental and disability benefits can be considered in relation to employee needs, business size, plan design and budget. Features vary by provider and plan.

[Explore Employee Benefits →](/services/employee-benefits/)

## Registered Investment Options

RRSPs, TFSAs, FHSAs, RESPs and RDSPs have different purposes, eligibility rules, contribution rules, tax treatment and withdrawal considerations.

[Explore Registered Investment Options →](/services/registered-investments/)

## Non-Registered Investment Options

Non-registered options may become relevant after considering liquidity, time horizon, risk tolerance, taxation and available registered room. Product availability depends on applicable licensing and compliance requirements.

[Explore Non-Registered Investment Options →](/services/non-registered-investments/)

## Insurance Policy Reviews

Existing policies can be reviewed for coverage, ownership, beneficiaries, renewal and conversion features, premium structure, riders and changes in family, employment, income, debt or business circumstances.
