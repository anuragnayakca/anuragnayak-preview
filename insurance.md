## Life Insurance
Life insurance can help address responsibilities such as family income needs, debts, mortgage obligations, business needs and estate objectives. Term and permanent insurance serve different purposes, and the appropriate structure depends on the situation.

## Critical Illness Insurance
Critical illness insurance may provide a lump-sum benefit when the insured is diagnosed with a covered condition and satisfies the requirements of the policy. Covered conditions, definitions and survival periods vary by contract. Certain policies may offer optional return-of-premium features.

## Disability Insurance
Disability insurance is designed to help replace a portion of income when illness or injury prevents an insured person from working, subject to the policy definition. Waiting periods, benefit periods, occupational definitions and coordination with workplace coverage all matter.

## Personal Mortgage Protection
Personally owned life or disability coverage and lender or creditor insurance can protect mortgage-related obligations in different ways. Ownership, portability, beneficiary structure, underwriting and cost should be compared rather than assuming one approach is always better.

## Common questions about personal insurance
### Term or permanent life insurance?
They are designed for different objectives. Term insurance can be useful for needs with a defined period, while permanent insurance may be considered for longer-term or estate objectives. Cost, duration, flexibility and the reason for coverage should drive the comparison.

### Can workplace life insurance replace personal coverage?
It may cover some or all of a person's need. The important questions are the amount provided, whether it changes with salary or employment, conversion options and whether the coverage remains in place if the person leaves the employer.

### How does critical illness insurance pay?
A benefit may be payable when the insured meets the policy definition for a covered condition and any applicable survival period. Definitions, exclusions, covered conditions and optional features vary by policy.

### What should I compare in disability insurance?
Look beyond the monthly benefit. Waiting period, benefit period, occupational definition, exclusions, integration with workplace benefits and whether premiums or benefits receive particular tax treatment can all matter.

### Will every applicant qualify for the same coverage or price?
No. Availability, underwriting and pricing depend on factors such as age, health, occupation, smoking status, amount of coverage and the insurer's rules.
