## What group extended health benefits can include
Depending on the carrier, business size, eligibility and plan design, a group benefits package may include:

- Extended health care
- Prescription drug coverage
- Dental coverage
- Vision care
- Paramedical services
- Travel or emergency medical benefits
- Group life or disability benefits where available

Features, limits, eligibility and employer/employee cost sharing vary by plan.

## Start with the business and the people
There is no single benefits package that fits every employer. A useful review looks at the size and makeup of the team, recruitment and retention goals, budget, current benefits, employee expectations and how much administration the business wants to manage.

## For businesses adding benefits for the first time
If you do not currently offer a plan, I can help you understand the available structures, what information is needed to obtain options and the trade-offs between richer coverage and cost.

## For businesses reviewing an existing plan
If you already have group benefits, a review can help you understand the current plan design, changes in premiums or usage, employee needs and whether alternative structures are worth considering.

## Keep business protection separate but connected
Employee benefits, key-person protection, shareholder funding and the owner's personal insurance solve different problems. They can be reviewed together without treating them as one product decision.
